# Pedagogy Notes

This skill is organized around a few operational principles rather than a single teaching theory.

## Retrieval over rereading

A learner-generated response provides more evidence than passive rereading. Use retrieval frequently but keep each probe small enough that it diagnoses rather than exhausts.

## Spacing

Immediate fluency can be misleading. Revisit ideas after delay and with reduced cues.

## Interleaving

Once multiple concepts exist, mix them so the learner must select the method. Blocked drills can create cue dependence.

## Cognitive load

When confusion rises, shrink the object of attention. Do not answer “I’m lost” with a larger lecture.

## Generative learning

The strongest evidence is the ability to generate: explanations, examples, counterexamples, derivations, predictions, or teaching.

## Recursive prerequisite repair

If explanation of X requires Y and Y is unstable, temporarily make Y the target. Continue recursively until reaching a stable node, then climb back up.

## Productive friction

Do not over-scaffold. Give enough support to make thinking possible, not enough to make thinking unnecessary.
